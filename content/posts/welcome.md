Welcome to my corner of the web. I'm Milad Sefidyan, a lawyer now based in Milan, working where law meets finance and sustainability.

This site brings my [CV](/cv/), [projects](/projects/) and [education](/education/) together in one place, in English, Italian and Persian. The blog is where I'll think out loud.

## What I'll write about

- **Financial regulation and compliance**: how rules shape banks and companies, and what compliance looks like in highly regulated sectors.
- **ESG and sustainability law**: EU sustainability frameworks, the Sustainable Development Goals, and what it takes for a company to move from commitments to compliance.
- **Geopolitical risk and the economy**: notes from my LL.M. research on how geopolitical risk relates to growth and macroeconomic stability.
- **Law and data**: empirical legal studies, and using quantitative tools to answer legal and economic questions.

## Why three languages?

Persian is my native language, English is the language I work in, and Italian is the language of the country where I live. Some posts will appear in all three, others in just one.
